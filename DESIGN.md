# Kiwari Coffee design direction

Reading this as: mobile-first home coffee ordering storefront for local buyers, in a tactile coffee-menu language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- **Purpose:** get a buyer from menu discovery to a complete WhatsApp order with as little interpretation as possible.
- **Audience:** local customers ordering from a phone; they scan names, sizes, prices, and special requests first.
- **Tone:** warm, handmade, editorial, grounded in the supplied Kiwari menu artwork.
- **Palette:** espresso black, coffee brown, cream paper, and orange-brown accent mirror the supplied cup and bottle menu artwork.
- **Typography:** Oswald gives the menu its condensed poster voice; DM Sans keeps prices, forms, and checkout readable.
- **Composition:** menu is the primary content, not a generic marketing feature grid. The dark process section separates the operational story from browsing.
- **Motion:** only short hover and panel transitions are used, so ordering remains calm and usable on a phone.
- **Assets:** supplied logo and menu artwork are used as provided. Product photos were not supplied, so menu cards use an honest abstract cup visual rather than fabricated product photography.
- **Temporary configuration:** WhatsApp number in `app.js` is a clearly marked placeholder and must be replaced before publishing.
